Objects changed ? An object is a single OSM map element — a node, way, or relation (a point, a road/building outline, or a group of other elements). This counts how many objects were added, modified, or deleted across changesets — a rough measure of actual mapping work, since one changeset can touch anywhere from 1 to 10,000 objects.

Changeset size ? A changeset's size is the number of objects it changed. Most changesets are tiny (one tag fix, one StreetComplete answer), while a few (imports, bots, mass edits) change thousands of objects each. The median and percentiles describe the typical changeset; the average is pulled up by the few large ones.
